Our Services

STANRIM CORP. provides modern, reliable tools for dry cleaners and laundromats. Our flagship POS system, CleanBaseV, is designed to streamline operations, improve customer communication, and support business growth.

CleanBaseV POS System (Primary Service)

CleanBaseV is a full‑featured Point‑of‑Sale system built specifically for dry cleaners and laundromats. It includes:

• Order creation and tracking
• Customer management
• Payment processing
• Pickup & delivery workflow
• Inventory and garment tagging
• Multi‑store support
• Reporting and business analytics

We offer CleanBaseV as a rental service, providing installation, updates, and ongoing support.

SMS Notification System (Built‑In POS Feature)

CleanBaseV includes integrated SMS messaging to help businesses communicate with customers quickly and professionally.

• Order ready notifications
• Pickup reminders
• Delay or issue alerts
• Customer service messaging

SMS is fully automated through the POS and uses your verified business number.

Technical Support & Maintenance

We provide ongoing support for CleanBaseV, including software updates, troubleshooting, and system enhancements to ensure your business runs smoothly.
