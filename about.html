About STANRIM CORP.

Who We Are

STANRIM CORP. is a technology company focused on building modern, reliable tools for dry cleaners and laundromats. Our flagship product, CleanBaseV, is a full‑featured Point‑of‑Sale system designed specifically for the garment‑care industry.

Our Main Business: CleanBaseV POS

CleanBaseV is the core of our business. We design, develop, and rent this POS system to dry cleaners and laundromats who need a dependable, industry‑specific solution for managing daily operations.

CleanBaseV includes:
• Order creation and tracking
• Customer management
• Payment processing
• Pickup & delivery workflow
• Inventory and garment tagging
• Multi‑store support
• Reporting and analytics

SMS Messaging: A Built‑In POS Feature

SMS messaging is an integrated feature of CleanBaseV. It allows dry cleaners and laundromats to communicate with customers quickly and professionally.

CleanBaseV automatically sends:
• Order ready notifications
• Pickup reminders
• Delay or issue alerts
• Customer service messages

SMS is not a separate service — it is part of the POS system we provide.

Our Commitment

We are committed to supporting small and medium‑sized dry‑cleaning and laundry businesses with tools that improve workflow, enhance customer experience, and streamline operations. CleanBaseV is continuously updated and improved to meet the evolving needs of the industry.
